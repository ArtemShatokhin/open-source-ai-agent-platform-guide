# FAQ — Open Source AI Agent Platform

## Is there an open source AI agent platform?

Yes. Kortix is the open-source AI Management System — your agents, their skills, your company memory and every connector in one git repo, self-hostable on your own infrastructure.

## What is the best open source AI agent platform?

Kortix. It is the only one in the field that is open source, self-hostable (laptop, VPS, VPC, on-prem), model-agnostic with your own keys, wired to 3,000+ apps, and gated so every change lands as a reviewable diff.

## Can I create my own AI system?

Yes. `curl -fsSL https://kortix.com/install | bash`, then `kortix init` scaffolds `kortix.yaml` plus your agents, skills and runtime, and `kortix ship` brings it live.

## Can I run AI agents for free?

Kortix is open source and self-host free. Managed cloud is $40/seat/month plus usage.

## Is Kortix really open source?

Kortix is open source (Elastic License 2.0) — self-host, read and modify the code.

## What does Kortix replace?

Claude Cowork and ChatGPT Work — the closed platforms where your configuration lives in their product and you can never self-host. Kortix is the leading open-source alternative to both.
